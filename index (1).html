Lightweight encryption algorithm for cloud data security

MSc dissertation, Staffordshire University, 2024

Cloud workloads need encryption that protects data without slowing systems down. I designed a lightweight multi-round algorithm built on key expansion and layered XOR operations, implemented it in MATLAB, and benchmarked it against AES and DES on files from 10 KB to 500 KB. Each test was averaged over 20 runs, and I also evaluated the avalanche effect and plaintext–ciphertext correlation.

Proposed algorithmEstablished algorithms

On small files the algorithm ran about 30% faster than AES, and at 200 KB it stayed well ahead of AES and close to DES. AES remains the standard for strong security. This work explored whether a lighter design can cut processing cost for resource-limited cloud workloads; formal cryptanalysis would be the next step before any real deployment.
